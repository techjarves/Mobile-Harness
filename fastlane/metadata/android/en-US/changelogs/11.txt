Agent updates gain a second trust anchor: a project-controlled feed signed with an offline Ed25519 key (minisign format), verified on device with a self-contained RFC 8032 implementation — so a new agent version can be vouched for by signing a manifest instead of shipping an app release. Verification is fail-closed at every step (unknown key, tampered byte, non-https URL, malformed digest), and the channel stays dormant until the project's signing key is activated per docs/release/signing-agent-updates.md. The per-release pinned-digest allowlist remains the fallback anchor, exactly as before.
