Backup & Restore in Settings: save projects, files, chats and settings into one file.
A backup password also saves API keys and logins (GitHub, git, SSH, coding agents).
Restoring adds the backup's projects next to the ones on your phone.
After a reinstall, restore from the setup screen to get everything back.
Backups keep running in the background and show live progress.
A What's New screen shows after each update.
