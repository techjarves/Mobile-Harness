Interrupted tasks are no longer lost when Android kills the app mid-job: the active task is journaled to disk, and the next start shows a "Task interrupted — Resume / Dismiss" banner (plus a system notification). Resume continues the agent's own conversation for Claude Code (--resume) and Antigravity (--conversation); DeepSeek Harness rebuilds from the saved transcript. After repeated interruptions the banner offers a direct shortcut to the battery-optimization exemption list, since aggressive power management is the usual culprit.
